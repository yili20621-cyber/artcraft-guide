Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version

ArtCraftGuide is a static content site. We don't have accounts, we don't sell data, and we don't collect personal information beyond standard server logs and — if enabled — privacy-respecting analytics and advertising cookies.

What we collect

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and operations.
  • Analytics (if enabled). We may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic — page views, rough geography, device type. No personal profiles are built.
  • Advertising cookies (if enabled). We may show ads via Google AdSense, which uses cookies to serve and measure ads. You can opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email, or any account data (no accounts exist).
  • Your files. Any tools we link to (e.g., PhotoCraft's browser build) process files locally on your device — nothing passes through us.

Third-party links

We link to external sites (getartcraft.com, github.com, Adobe, Photopea, and others). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Your choices

  • Block cookies in your browser — the site works fine without them.
  • Opt out of personalized ads via Google's ad settings.
  • Contact us (see Contact) with any privacy question.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
